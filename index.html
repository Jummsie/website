About Us

Grace family International Church was founded by the Senior Pastors, Revs. Yinka and Deola Ojo, in 1990. The work of the ministry was inaugurated in the city of Ibadan where the first congregation was planted. Since then, the scope of the ministry has extended to many other nations and the impact continues to grow. Evangelistic and teaching outreaches, church planting, CDs and book publication ministry, outreach to the poor (Love & Care), and Television broadcasts are just some of the ways our church is carrying out the God-given vision (Act 26:19).
